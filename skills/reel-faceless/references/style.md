# Faceless reel defaults

These came from Eren's v1 → v5 feedback on his reel. They are the preset's defaults; preferences in `CLAUDE.md` override them.

1. **Calm pacing:** 2–4 s shots; at most ~2 soft punch-ins; no continuous zoom.
2. **Never touch footage colour,** brightness or contrast. No gradients or flashes.
3. **Every pop-in gets a sound effect.** No whooshes.
4. **Calm captions:** fade-in only, newest word yellow, no bounce.
5. **Safe margins:** UI elements ≥ 100 px from the left edge; captions around y = 1300 on 9:16; thumbnail content inside the centre 1080×1350.
6. **Official external assets** (logos, product images) rather than crops of the footage.
7. **Always a thumbnail** when ticked.
8. **Faceless:** never suggest or plan face or talking-head content for this preset.

What went wrong before, so you don't repeat it:

| Version | What Eren said | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | Too many cuts, too fast, too many zooms, dizzying | 2–4 s shots, 2 soft punch-ins max |
| v2 | Looks dark and artificial | Never grade; the app's export keeps BT.709 |
| v5 | Captions too bouncy and tiring | Fade only |
| thumb | "Say ücretsiz, not bedava" | Use the user's words in headlines |
