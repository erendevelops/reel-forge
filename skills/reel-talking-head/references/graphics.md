## Layouts

- `full-face`: the hook line, personal asides, the outro. Cards there sit in the band under the captions.
- `split`: graphics in the top half, the face as a wide 4:3 rounded card in the bottom half (the compiler frames a portrait take to the card at the full source width; never a zoom). Most of the body.
- `full-graphics`: whole frame for a concept scene (an illustrated metaphor, an app window doing the work, a big comparison). 2–6 s.
- `broll`: the creator's own cutaways, only if the take has them and the layout is ticked.

## Captions

Word-build captions sit at 68 % of the height: over the face card in split scenes (as onur.builds does), over the
face in full-face scenes. Keep caption groups short (1–3 words) so they never compete with the graphics, and keep
graphics in `full-graphics` scenes out of the caption band (y 1280–1480 on 9:16) or plan that stretch with few words.
